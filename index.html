Under The Hood

Dynamic Pooling & Corridor Matching Engine

How SahiRide evaluates incoming requests in real-time, optimizes multi-stop routes, ensures vector alignment, and fairly splits fares without fixed bus stops.

01

Vector Cosine Alignment

Evaluates directional similarity between passenger vector \(\vec{u}\) and driver route \(\vec{v}\). Requires \(\cos \theta \ge 0.82\) (\(\theta \le 35^\circ\)) so co-riders never travel backwards.

02

Corridor Detour Bounding

Constructs an \(\epsilon\)-radius buffer (default 1.5 km) around driver's return trajectory. Pickup and drop points must project within this buffer.

03

Dynamic Insertion (TSP)

Calculates minimal marginal detour time \(\Delta t\) for candidate pickup/drop permutations while strictly enforcing legal vehicle seating capacity (\(\le 3\) for auto, \(\le 4\) for cab).

04

Fair Segment Pricing & Promos

Each passenger pays based on individual traveled segment with upfront pool discounts, while the platform sponsors free trial onboarding rides for new riders.
